Start with facts, not guesses:
  helm history checkout -n helmissions
  helm get values checkout -n helmissions --revision 1   # and 2, 3
Then see what actually differs between the release and the live Deployment:
  helm get manifest checkout -n helmissions | grep -A1 'name: PAYMENT'
  kubectl get deploy checkout -n helmissions -o jsonpath='{.spec.template.spec.containers[0].env}'
